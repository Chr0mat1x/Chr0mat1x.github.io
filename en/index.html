FAQ

How much does a website cost?

Roughly $150–300 depending on scope and complexity. After a short brief we quote an exact price — it's fixed in the estimate and never grows along the way.

Who writes the copy and designs?

We do the design ourselves. Copywriting is on us too, or we can work with your content — let's discuss at the brief.

How does payment work?

In milestones: 50% upfront, the rest after acceptance. Flexible on contracts and payment methods.

Can I update the site myself?

Yes. We hook up a user-friendly admin panel and record a short walkthrough. Or just message us — we'll help.

I have a site but it's outdated

We'll audit it and honestly say whether a redesign or a touch-up makes more sense. We also take projects on support and speed them up.

Let's talk?
